Common Questions

Everything you need to know before starting

Do I need a developer to set it up?

No. We set it up for you. You just need access to your store and Printful. The full setup takes less than 30 minutes and we guide you through every step.

Does it work with Printful + Shopify or WooCommerce?

Yes, it works with both. Inbox Zero connects directly to Printful webhooks, so it doesn't matter which platform you use for your store.

What if the customer doesn't receive the email?

Every email includes a tracking link that the customer can save and check whenever they want. Plus, we send multiple emails at each stage of the order to maximize delivery.

Will my customers know the emails come from a third party?

No. Emails are sent with your brand. We include your logo, colors, and domain. To your customers, it looks like you're sending them directly.

How long does setup take?

Between 20 and 30 minutes. We schedule a call, connect your Printful, configure the emails with your brand, and do a test order. Ready for production the same day.
